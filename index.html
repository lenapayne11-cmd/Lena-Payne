About Me

Hi, I am Lena Payne.

Book marketer, lifelong reader, and your book's most passionate advocate.

I started my journey in book marketing because I kept seeing the same heartbreaking pattern: incredibly talented authors writing extraordinary books that never found their audience. Not because the books were not good enough. But because nobody taught these authors how to get their work in front of the right readers.

On the other side, I have cultivated a massive community of real, engaged readers who trust my recommendations and buy books I feature.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Honest answers to the questions authors ask most often.

How does working with you get my book in front of readers?

+

I use a multi channel approach: my 50,000+ reader community segmented by genre, targeted social media campaigns on BookTok and Bookstagram, Amazon optimization, promotional placements on BookBub and similar platforms, and strategic newsletter cross promotions.

What genres do you work with?

+

Romance, thriller, science fiction, fantasy, literary fiction, memoir, self help, business, health, wellness, parenting, children's, Christian fiction, and nonfiction. My reader community spans all these genres.

How much do your services cost?

+

Every book is different, so services are customized. I offer flexible options from focused packages to VIP partnerships. You receive a detailed proposal with no hidden fees before we begin.

How soon will I see results?

+

Most authors see measurable results within 30 to 60 days. Amazon ranking improvements can happen within two weeks. Sustained growth builds over three to six months as strategies compound.

What if my book is already published and did not sell well?

+

This is one of my specialties. Most underperforming books have nothing wrong with them. The problem is almost always visibility and positioning. Some of my biggest successes are revival campaigns.

What makes you different from other book marketers?

+

Three things: my built in reader community of 50,000+, the limited number of authors I take on for personal attention, and radical transparency in everything I do. You always know what is happening and why.

About Me

Hi, I am Lena Payne.

Book marketer, lifelong reader, and your book's most passionate advocate.

I started my journey in book marketing because I kept seeing the same heartbreaking pattern: incredibly talented authors writing extraordinary books that never found their audience. Frequently Asked Questions

Honest, detailed answers to the questions authors ask me most often.

How does working with you actually get my book in front of readers?

+

I use a multi channel approach: my 50,000+ reader community segmented by genre, targeted social media campaigns on BookTok and Bookstagram, Amazon optimization, promotional placements on platforms like BookBub, and strategic newsletter cross promotions.

What genres do you work with?

+

I work across romance, thriller, science fiction, fantasy, literary fiction, memoir, self help, business, health, wellness, parenting, psychology, spiritual growth, and nonfiction.

How much do your services cost?

+

Every book is different, so my services are customized. I offer flexible options from focused packages to comprehensive VIP partnerships. I provide a detailed proposal with no hidden fees.

How soon will I see results?

+

Most authors see measurable results within 30 to 60 days. Some results like Amazon ranking improvements can happen within two weeks. Sustained growth builds over three to six months.

What if my book is already published and did not sell well?

+

This is one of my specialties. Most books that do not sell well have nothing wrong with the book itself. The problem is almost always visibility and positioning.

What makes you different from other book marketers?

+

Three things: my built in reader community of 50,000+, the limited number of authors I work with for personal attention, and radical transparency in everything I do.
